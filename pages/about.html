Made slowly. Made to stay.

We make bags and accessories from warm materials and simple shapes, so they look good today and still look good in ten years.

How it started

Umber & Rose began on one small table, with one bag and a big question: why do we throw away things that could last?

We chose leather that ages well, colors taken from clay and flowers, and makers who are paid fairly. Every piece goes through the hands of people who care about it.
